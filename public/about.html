About this archive

I have collected material connected to My Beautiful Demons for a long time. At first it was only for myself, because there was no obvious place for these things to live and because small bands can disappear from memory very easily.

I am not trying to make a complete history. I do not have that, and I do not want to pretend that I do. What I have is a set of recordings, clippings, photographs, notes, and references that seemed worth keeping.

The pages here will probably stay uneven. Some entries will be clear. Some will have missing dates or uncertain sources. When I know something, I will say it. When I do not, I will leave room for correction.

How items are handled

I am trying to include a source note with each item when possible. That may be a person, a venue, a folder name, a date written on the back, or simply where I found it. If something is uncertain, I will mark it that way.

If you recognize something here later and can help place it more accurately, I would like that. I would rather correct the archive than protect a wrong guess.

This page will change as the archive gets less scattered.
