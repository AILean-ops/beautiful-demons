Home

This is a collection of material related to the band My Beautiful Demons. I started gathering what I could years ago. I am putting it here so it is not lost.

Some of it is ordinary. A few recordings, some paper, names on flyers, photographs where the date is not always certain. I have kept it because it felt like someone should.

The site is still small. I am adding things as I finish checking them and as I can say, even roughly, where they came from.

Nothing here is meant to be definitive. This is just the beginning of a public place for material that has mostly been sitting in folders, boxes, and old drives.
